How the sharing works

  • When something is kept, you get it too. A kept build usually becomes a skill in the PSD AI Agent or a tool in AI Studio, and its Wrap-Up links straight to it — so you can use the thing, not just read about it.
  • Inside PSD: full Wrap-Ups, visible to all staff. The Monday Summary gives cabinet five bullets on what’s moving, what’s stuck, and where teams are converging. Each quarter, the Quarterly Story pulls the whole cycle into one narrative.
  • Outside PSD: public versions of Wrap-Ups (student and staff details removed) publish at psd401.ai. Other districts are welcome to borrow anything — that’s why we share. We’d rather help ten districts skip our dead ends than protect a tidy image.

Why we publish the flops

Most organizations quietly drop what doesn’t work, and the learning dies in silence. Here, a stopped build that saves other teams from the same dead end counts as a win. If you only ever see successes on this page, the model is broken — tell us.
