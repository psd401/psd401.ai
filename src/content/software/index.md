# 02 Software

Products the district builds and runs, all open source.

Concept type: `software` · 6 entries

## Concepts

- [AI Studio](/software/ai-studio.md) - Frontier AI models for every teacher and student in the district, behind your own authentication, at about a tenth of per-seat cost.
- [Atrium & Atrium Capture](/software/atrium.md) _(draft)_ - Structured capture of classroom and meeting evidence.
- [LessonLens](/software/lessonlens.md) _(draft)_ - Reads a lesson plan and shows the gaps against our Instructional Essentials.
- [PSD AI Agents](/software/psd-ai-agents.md) _(draft)_ - District agents that know our policies, our data and our calendars.
- [PSD EOC](/software/psd-eoc.md) _(draft)_ - Emergency operations support — status, communications drafts, and a decision log.
- [PSD PRR Records](/software/psd-prr-records.md) _(draft)_ - Public records requests, triaged and redacted with a human in the loop.
