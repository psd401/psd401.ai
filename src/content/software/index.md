# 02 Software

Products the district builds and runs, all open source.

Concept type: `software` · 7 entries

## Concepts

* [AI Studio](/software/ai-studio.md) - Frontier AI models for every staff member and student in the district, behind district sign-in, at about a tenth of the cost of per-seat licences.
* [Atrium Capture](/software/atrium-capture.md) - Record a workflow in Chrome or on a Mac and turn it into an editable step-by-step guide, saved to Atrium as a private draft — without ever capturing a password.
* [Atrium](/software/atrium.md) - A workspace in AI Studio where staff and their agents write documents and build pages together, then publish them to the intranet or the web.
* [LessonLens](/software/lessonlens.md) - A private coaching tool for teachers — record or import a lesson and get AI feedback on specific teaching techniques that no administrator or evaluator can see.
* [PSD AI Agents](/software/psd-ai-agents.md) - A personal AI agent for district staff, with its own district Google account, that works on its owner's behalf in Google Chat — the way an assistant would.
* [PSD EOC](/software/psd-eoc.md) - Emergency notification and operations for districts: start an incident or drill, notify staff, work it in a live timeline, keep an append-only record.
* [PSD PRR Records](/software/psd-prr-records.md) - Our public records request system, built to replace NextRequest — Washington's deadlines, exemptions and audit trail enforced in code rather than by policy.
